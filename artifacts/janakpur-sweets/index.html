Our Story

About Janakpur Sweets

🍮

Nestled in the heart of Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Janakpur Sweets brings the authentic, time-honoured taste of Mithila right to your neighbourhood.

Every day, our kitchen fills with the aroma of freshly fried samosas, golden jalebis, and hand-crafted mithai made from recipes passed down through generations. We believe great food starts with fresh ingredients, prepared with love and served with a smile.

Our cooperative staff and welcoming owner ensure every visit feels like coming home. Whether you're grabbing a quick samosa on the way to work or picking up a box of assorted mithai for a celebration — you'll always find something irresistible here.
